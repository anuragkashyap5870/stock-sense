Inventory & Warehouse Operations

Digitize stock registers, automate double-entry movements, and streamline warehouse fulfillment. Equipped with real-time receipts, delivery orders, internal rack transfers, and instant WebRTC camera barcode scanning.

Architected & Engineered by Anurag Kashyap & Team

Incoming Receipts

Receive goods from vendors, add product lines, and click Validate to automatically increase warehouse stock levels.

Outgoing Deliveries

Customer order picking and packing with automatic real-time stock deduction upon delivery dispatch.

Internal Transfers & Scrap

Move goods between Main Store, Production Rack, and Rack B. Write off damaged inventory with reasons into scrap ledger.

Live Camera Scanner

Hardware WebRTC camera video scanning with camera flip, image barcode decoding, and instant 1-click SKU test presets.

Move History Ledger

Color-coded audit log (Green IN, Red OUT) preserving immutable records of every single unit moved across locations.
